French Fries

Description

This is how u make french fries

Ingredients :

  • Potatoes
  • Salt
  • Oil

Steps :

  1. Peel the Potatoes
  2. Wash the Potatoes
  3. Let them dry
  4. After they dry, sprinkle some salt on them
  5. Put oil in the fryer pot on high heat
  6. Let it heat
  7. Put the fries in
  8. Let them fry
  9. When theyre good, put them in the drainer
  10. Sprinkle some salt

Congrats now you made the most basic fries ever
