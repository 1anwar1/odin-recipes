Red Tuna

Description

This is how u make Red Tuna

Ingredients :

  • Tuna
  • Tomato sauce
  • Onion
  • Oil
  • Optional : any vegetables

Steps :

  1. Slice the onions into small slices
  2. Put a little oil on a pan
  3. Turn on medium heat then put the small sliced onions
  4. Stir the onion until it becomes golden
  5. Put the tomato sauce then mix then put the tuna then mix
